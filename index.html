📐 Efficiency Score Methodology

Formula: Eff. Score = ROAS × 50% + CTR × 30% + (1/CPM) × 20% Step 1 Normalize each metric to 0–1 scale (divide by max value across all use cases; CPM is inverted so lower = better) Step 2 Weight: ROAS 50% (revenue efficiency) · CTR 30% (creative appeal) · CPM 20% (cost efficiency) Step 3 Rank by composite score. Higher = more efficient use case overall. Note: Score helps compare relative efficiency but should be read alongside absolute spend and revenue. A high score on low spend means "promising but unproven at scale".
