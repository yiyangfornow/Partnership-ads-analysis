ZG USP Testing — April 2026

19 ads across 7 use case angles in the KOC Others ad set. Total spend €401, revenue €2,754.

KOC Content Deepdive (n=80 videos, Feb–Apr 2026)

All 80 KOC videos manually tagged across 9 creative dimensions. 47.5% had zero conversions — top 25 converters (ROAS>3) drive the key signals.

Key Findings

Video Style

Demo ROAS 5.50 — product in action converts best (16% of videos, 50% convert).
Skit/Scenario (46%) workhorse: ROAS 3.43, €54.7K revenue, 45% convert.
Vlog (2.50) and Before/After (2.56) lag.

Hook Type

Story Hook #1: ROAS 5.30, 65% conversion rate (highest of all hooks).
Pain Point reliable: ROAS 3.67, €39.5K revenue, 39% convert.
Result-first (0.81, 17% convert) and Shock/Surprise (0.95, 25%) — avoid.

Tone & Placement

Funny (n=7): ROAS 5.28 vs Casual 3.31.
Soft sell 4.58 vs Hard sell 3.27.
Explicit CTA 4.76 vs Soft CTA 3.00 — soft placement + direct CTA = best combo.

Winning Formula

Story Hook + Skit or Demo + Soft sell + Explicit CTA

Ice cream visible: ROAS 11.4 vs 3.0. Fast opening: 3.85 vs 3.08.
Best cross-tab: Skit × Story = 5.65 (n=12). Demo × Pain Point = 19.1 (n=3).

Additional Insights

Character Archetype

Solo creator (n=34): ROAS 4.13 — simplicity wins.
Couple (n=5): ROAS 5.14 — relationship context resonates.
With Elders (n=12): ROAS 2.02 — elder-featuring content underperforms despite Parent Gifting being a top use case. Story quality matters more than casting.

Product Performance

Futon套組 (n=3): ROAS 7.59 — small but strong.
Hybrid套組 (n=14): ROAS 4.52, €53.5K revenue — the volume driver.
Diamond套組 (n=16): ROAS 3.50, €43.9K revenue — steady performer.
ZG套組 (n=17): ROAS 4.34 — promising given it's new.
